# MIV release-control review — proposal, not release approval

Scope: standalone MIV on chin.hambelelaorganic.com. No change to deployment, hosting settings, credentials or app files.

## Finding

The deploy workflow supplies a fixed SHA-256 for the previous purchase planner release. The deploy script recomputes the six-file digest and rejects other bytes. This identifies approved bytes, but the digest carries no evidence of who approved them or what scanner examined them. FastComet ticket GOI-457-19250 describes on-upload scanning and no manual pre-deployment clearance.

## Evidence for PR #93

- Candidate commit: `3f4ec1748bf58d66cc97088d8d7d18cd5850b85b`.
- [Login, logout and account tests](https://github.com/victoriatoivo-hash/hambelela-portal/actions/runs/41636181455) passed. The PR was not deployed by that run.
- Six candidate files matched digest `57b37630fc031ed18903d9eaa4e0943c0604929115f8f818fa7728c012dfc2d8`. Host cPanel ClamAV 1.5.4 reported six files scanned, zero infected on 2026-11-03 at 13:13 UTC. This is not an Imunify360 result or a FastComet approval.
- At last inspection no completed Imunify360 result covering the staged candidate had been verified. No production clearance is asserted here.

## Decision for the repository maintainer

Specify the acceptable scanner, freshness and evidence for authentication changes, and who independently reviews them. If the required evidence is unavailable, the release stays blocked. Document that decision separately from #93; neither this document nor merging it clears a release.

For a future implementation, bind a verifiable scan result to the exact candidate digest and require approval by a designated reviewer independent of the change author before production credentials become available. Recompute the digest immediately before upload. Reject missing or mismatched evidence and changed bytes; never accept a digest generated by the job itself as proof of a scan. Keep tests, TLS, destination marker, predecessor checks, read-back and rollback. Treat on-upload provider monitoring as a separate post-upload verification step.

Acceptance tests for any proposed rule change: missing evidence, stale evidence, altered artifact and missing independent approval each prevent all writes; only the independently reviewed exact artifact can advance. Until the maintainer adopts and validates a policy, retain the existing deployment rule.
